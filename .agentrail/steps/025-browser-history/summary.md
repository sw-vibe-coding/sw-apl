Up and down walk the history in the browser REPL, as rustyline does at the CLI, including giving back a half-typed line when walking down past the end. Blank lines and immediate repeats are not kept. Implemented in the page because ArrowUp and ArrowDown were unclaimed by apl-board's act, so nothing below the terminal had to change. Physical keyboard only, as asked; no board control. History lasts the tab; surviving a reload was not asked for and was not done.
