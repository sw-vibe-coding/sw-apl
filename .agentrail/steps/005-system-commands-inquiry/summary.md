Moved )SI and )SIV into it too, since they report what the state indicator holds. Researched the IBM APL/360 User's Manual (Aug 1968) directly rather than working from memory: it settled that )FNS, )VARS and )GRPS print alphabetically from a letter while )GRP does not sort, that )GROUP replies nothing and reports NOT GROUPED, NAME IN USE, and that )ERASE leaves functions on the state indicator and replies NOT ERASED: with their names. It also showed that NOT SAVED, THIS WS IS wsid takes a comma, so the colon I introduced in step 004 on GNU APL's wording is reverted; the rule is a comma before a reason and a colon before a list.
