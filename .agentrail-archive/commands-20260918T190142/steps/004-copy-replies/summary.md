Gave )COPY and )PCOPY the replies the manual specifies. Both printed nothing before, whether they copied everything, something or nothing. Now both print the SAVED line the source records, as )LOAD does, and )PCOPY names what it would not overwrite: NOT COPIED: A. That second line is the one that mattered in substance rather than form -- a protected copy exists to leave alone what you already hold, and without the report it could not be told from one that had nothing to leave. An ordinary )COPY overwrites and so never keeps anything, which the manual reflects by giving WC3 only the first line, and which is now a test of its own rather than an accident.
