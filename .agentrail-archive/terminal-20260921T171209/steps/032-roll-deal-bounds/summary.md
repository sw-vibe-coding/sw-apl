Roll computes N times (link-1) in 128 bits, so large rolls are exact instead of silently wrapped: ?1E15 was 7,753,573,564 and is now 458,650,131,671,364. Deal is a sparse Fisher-Yates holding only swapped positions, so memory is the hand's rather than the pool's: about 6.5 MB for 1?1E9, which previously wanted 8 GB and escaped the workspace quota. The swaps are unchanged, proven against the old dense deal as an oracle, and reg-rs stayed 80 of 80 without a rebase. The link-to-range formula differs from J's by one at large N; recorded in docs/language.md and not changed. Large N past the link count is not refused, since no historical limit is established.
