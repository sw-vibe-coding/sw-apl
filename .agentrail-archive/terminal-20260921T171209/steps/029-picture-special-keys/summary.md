The keyboard picture shows the board's mode on every key via an adaptation, APL-keybd2-board.svg, generated from the original by scripts/board-keys.py --adapt: a class on each of the 88 glyphs saying which face it is on and on the parts of Caps Lock, pixel-identical to the original and recorded in ATTRIBUTION.md under CC BY-SA. Drawn inline so CSS bolds the active face and dims the other. The picture's wide keys, drawn as paths, are measured from their path data: Caps Lock switches APL and ABC and is lit in APL, Return sends the line, Tab and Shift inert. Pushed as cea1b91 and confirmed live.
