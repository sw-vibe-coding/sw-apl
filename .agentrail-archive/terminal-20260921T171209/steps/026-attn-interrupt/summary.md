ATTN for the interpreter, the CLI and the browser demo. A per-session flag in a new apl-attn crate, installed per thread so no primitive signature moved; polled between lines and, by the owner's rule that a tight loop be loose, inside every primitive via one helper in the scalar dispatcher, costing about seven percent on four million products. In a browser the flag is a new slot in the shared channel, raised by Escape, Ctrl-[ and a board ATTN key, cleared on every send so it cannot stop the next line. Idle blocks in Atomics.wait rather than spinning, verified by reading. The service half, aplterm, nc and the WebSocket page, is moved to its own step because the owner asked for the keyboard first and two of its three parts have real design problems.
