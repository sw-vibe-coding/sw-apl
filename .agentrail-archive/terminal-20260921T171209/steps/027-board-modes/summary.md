The board is the 2741's own keyboard: the CC BY-SA picture embedded unedited as an SVG image, with a hit region over each of its 45 keys measured out of the picture at build time by scripts/board-keys.py, resolving the group transforms that place them. Four modes via a segmented control -- APL and ABC as the two shift states of one picture, Commands and Idioms from data/board.json where a test runs every entry. ATTN top left, red, outdented wider; Return the biggest control; backspace on the picture overstrikes as on a 2741, Erase deletes. Commands and idioms insert and never send.
