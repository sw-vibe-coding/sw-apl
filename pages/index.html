sw-apl — an APL\360 session in this tab





  The interpreter is compiled to WebAssembly and runs in a worker in this
  tab. There is no server: nothing typed here leaves your browser, and
  nothing is sent anywhere. )SAVE and )LOAD have no
  filesystem to use yet. The keyboard is the IBM 2741's: unshifted letters
  are capitals, Shift-L is ⎕, and
  Ctrl-] overstrikes, so ⍟ is
  ○, that key, *. Hold Alt for the
  character a key is painted with. See docs/terminal.md for the whole
  board.
