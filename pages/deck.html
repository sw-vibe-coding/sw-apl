sw-apl U '74 University of Maryland APL, from punched cards

    A deck is a job: @RUN opens it, @APL calls APL, and @FIN ends it. Each card between is one APL statement in columns 1-72; columns 73-80 may hold a sequence number. The 029 has no APL characters, so a $ and two letters stand for one: $RH for rho, $IO for iota, $AL for the assignment arrow. Submit the deck and the printout lists each card as punched, the statement in APL beneath it, and what it printed.
