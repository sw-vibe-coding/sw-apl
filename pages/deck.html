    The $ spellings: what to punch for each APL character; choose one to punch it

    A deck is a job: @RUN opens it, @APL calls APL, and @FIN ends it. Each card between is one APL statement in columns 1-72; columns 73-80 may hold a sequence number. The 029 has no APL characters, so a $ and two letters stand for one: $RH for rho, $IO for iota, $AL for the assignment arrow. Submit the deck and the printout lists each card as punched, the statement in APL beneath it, and what it printed.

    Punch on the 029's keys or the computer's: a key punches its lower legend, or its upper one after NUMERIC. REL or Enter releases the card and feeds the next; FEED feeds a blank card after this one; DUP copies the column from the card before. Backspace and the left and right arrows move the punch; up and down move between cards. On the printout, choose a card or an error's CARD and COLUMN to go there and repunch it.

    To start from nothing, choose New deck: its control cards are punched, with a blank card between for the APL. The buttons punch a control card on a card of its own; an idiom punches the cards that do one thing, the ones after the vector using V.
